\noindent This master's thesis was written as part of the course 
TMM4935 ``Industrial ICT'' at the Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering 
at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU). 
The thesis accounts for 30 credits and was completed in the spring of 2026.

The thesis builds on my specialisation project, 
\textit{Supporting Method Selection for Machine Learning in Product Development and Production: A Multi-Dimensional Classification of Machine Learning Research} \parencite{bergsto2026mlselection}, 
completed in January 2026. 
The specialisation project performed an analysis of machine learning research that the present work draws on.
